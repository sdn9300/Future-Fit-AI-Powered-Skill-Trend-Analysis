### 9.9 Environmental Context — Coping Behaviour Update (Q4 Finding)

**The single most diagnostically significant finding of the entire follow-up round:**

When away from home — grandmother's house, travel, any novel environment with social presence — the compulsive coping behaviour drops "almost totally."

```
HOME ENVIRONMENT (alone):
  → Coping behaviour: near-daily, sometimes multiple times
  → Established physical cues + privacy + unstructured time
  → Familiar trigger locations, devices, times, and postures
  → A decade of co-occurrence has conditioned these associations

AWAY ENVIRONMENT (any novel context with social presence):
  → Coping behaviour: drops almost completely
  → Novel physical environment breaks established cue associations
  → Social presence removes the privacy trigger
  → Occupied with people or purposeful activity fills unstructured time
```

**What this restructures:**

The previous primary driver — "emotional state (stress, loneliness) is the primary cause" — is revised to:

**Primary driver: Environmental context and contextual conditioning.**
**Secondary driver: Emotional state amplifies frequency but is not the primary cause.**

A decade of co-occurring behavior in the home context has created **contextual cueing** — the home environment itself (specific rooms, devices, times, familiar postures) has become linked to the behavior pathway, triggering it before the emotional state fully develops. This is the same mechanism by which a specific smell can trigger a memory: the association is automatic.

**Revised intervention priority hierarchy:**

```
MOST EFFECTIVE (high leverage, low willpower requirement):
  ① Scheduled "away" sessions × 2 per week
     Library, café, park with laptop, any social visit
     Changes the physical context entirely — highest leverage

  ② In-home cue disruption
     Rearrange workspace, change device profiles, alter routines
     Disrupts the automatic contextual triggering

  ③ Social presence simulation when home-bound
     Focusmate, virtual co-working, video call with study partner
     Introduces perceived social presence without leaving home

SUPPLEMENTARY (helpful but lower leverage than above):
  ④ 5-minute pause + pre-chosen alternative (walk, sketch, music)
  ⑤ Distress tolerance practice (breathing, body scan)
  ⑥ Professional behavioral support at 6-month evaluation checkpoint
```

**The Q5 confirmation loop:** At grandmother's house, the coping behavior dropped AND the uncle conversation triggered a productive Linux learning cascade lasting multiple days. These are not coincidentally linked. Social engagement, novel environment, and purposeful activity were simultaneously protective against coping behavior AND generative of high-quality productive learning. The same environmental conditions do both jobs at once.

Current: "I'm now learning Linux, Docker, Kubernetes — which I'll also need for Conductor." All of these are genuinely relevant prerequisites. The pattern — discovering new legitimate content to fill the deferral space — is the diagnostic signal.
